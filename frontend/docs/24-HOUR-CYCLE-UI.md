# Night — Reflection UI Design

## Pulse of Nairobi

This document defines the UI/UX design and narrative intent for the **Night (9 PM – 4 AM)** section of _Pulse of Nairobi_, including the final wrap-up story.

## 1. Purpose

The Night section represents **stillness, absence, and reflection**.  
It is the emotional resolution of the project, where motion slows, noise fades, and the user is invited to interpret meaning rather than consume data.

This section prioritizes **restraint over density**.

## 2. Emotional Tone

- Calm
- Intimate
- Slightly uneasy
- Reflective

Design Principle:

> Silence is an interaction.

## 3. Entry Transition

### Scroll Trigger

When the user enters the Night section:

- Background transitions to deep navy / near-black
- Motion speed decelerates globally
- Ambient motion fades
- Clock becomes the dominant element

### Micro-copy (optional)

> _The city grows quiet._

## 4. Clock Design (Night Mode)

### Visual Changes

- Color saturation reduced
- Thin strokes only
- Seconds hand removed
- Subtle glow reduced or disabled

### Interaction

- Clock advances per scroll, not per second
- Final visible time: **03:59**

The clock communicates **time stretching**, not urgency.

## 5. Primary Visual Language

### Imagery Guidelines

- Empty streets
- Isolated streetlights
- Sparse apartment windows lit
- No crowds, no vehicles in motion

The focus is on **absence**, not activity.

## 6. Night Vitals (Minimal Set)

Night vitals are intentionally limited to avoid cognitive overload.

### Vital 1: Population Awake

- Metric: % of city still active
- Visual: Dots fading out over scroll
- Interpretation: Presence diminishing

### Vital 2: Energy Consumption Drop

- Metric: Electricity demand vs daily baseline
- Visual: Thin descending line
- Interpretation: City metabolism slowing

### Optional Vital 3: Emergency Activity

- Metric: Emergency calls / essential services
- Visual: Sparse, slow pulses
- Interpretation: Some systems never sleep

This metric must remain subtle and non-alarmist.

## 7. Data Visualization Rules (Night)

- No dashboards
- No bright colors
- One insight per scroll
- Emphasize negative space
- Long fade transitions

Night is not about explanation—it is about **interpretation**.

## 8. Scroll Behavior

- Slower scroll response
- Fewer triggered animations
- Forced pacing encouraged

The user should instinctively slow down.

## 9. Transition to Closing Story

At the end of Night:

- Clock fades out
- City motion pauses
- Background stabilizes into near-black

No sudden transitions.

## 10. Closing Story Section

### Headline

**A city is more than infrastructure.**

### Body Copy

Pulse of Nairobi explored the city as a living system—shaped by movement, pressure, rest, and resilience.

These patterns repeat every day.  
The question is whether we are listening.

Tone: reflective, non-prescriptive.

## 11. Optional Final Interaction

### Replay the Day

- Smooth scroll back to Morning (06:00)
- Clock rewinds gently
- Reinforces cyclical nature of urban life

## 12. Footer (Minimal)

- Data sources
- Tools used (D3.js, Scrollama)
- GitHub repository link

No heavy branding. No distraction.

## 13. Success Criteria

This section succeeds if:

- Users slow their scrolling
- Users pause before leaving
- Users scroll back up after finishing

The goal is **emotional resonance**, not retention metrics.

## 14. Design Philosophy Reminder

> End quietly.  
> Trust the user.  
> Let the city speak last.
